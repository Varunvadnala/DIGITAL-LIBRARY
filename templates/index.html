Two useful ways to learn

Normal Library vs Digital Library

A digital collection can complement a community center's physical library; it does not replace it.

FeatureNormal libraryDigital library
AccessVisit a physical location during opening hours.Open resources online when you have a device and connection.
SearchBrowse shelves or ask library staff.Search by title, author, and category.
StoragePrinted books and local shelves.Links and files hosted by resource providers.
AvailabilityDepends on location, hours, and available copies.Depends on internet access and provider availability.
CommunityOffers shared study space and in-person help.Offers remote access, but requires a device and connectivity.

Both formats have strengths and limits. This project is intended to support the community center's existing library.
